Findings in the revised implementation:
- **N20 [HIGH] `crates/hm-store/src/views.rs:119,183,188` — removal does not revoke task/decision effects and admits backdated writes into operational views.** An owner's observer grant removes open leases only. Existing tasks, task updates and decisions remain in `board` / `decisions`; room cards consume them without another role check (`crates/hm-brief/src/card.rs:40,49`), as does the feed task list (`crates/hm-daemon/src/feed.rs:393`).
  Concrete trace: member M creates a task/decision at L2/L3; owner removes M at L10. Both remain visible. After removal, M can sign a fresh task/decision/update with an unused sequence and L9: late-event rebuild (`crates/hm-store/src/store.rs:818`) applies it while M still has the default member role, then demotion leaves its effects intact. Direct brief filtering uses the final role (`crates/hm-brief/src/brief.rs:121`), so consumers disagree rather than ignoring M everywhere.
  Fix: make operational projections consistently enforce effective removal, retract/recompute task and decision contributions, and keep raw signed history separately. Test all three event families, including updates to another member's task, late lower-key delivery, rebuild/reopen and every view consumer. The new removal regression (`crates/hm-store/tests/lease.rs:122`) covers claims only.
- **N21 [MED] `crates/hm-store/src/settle.rs:207,216,222,313,354` — expired history still drives quadratic replay and linear state queries.** For N same-holder, disjoint claims with ts=T and TTL 60s, all expired at query time T+1day, replay retains every prior claim in `open`: stale=T-5min never reaches their expiry. Coexistence/parent scans do N(N-1)/2 comparisons (49,995,000 for 10,000 claims), despite no live claims.
  Independently, a long expired renewal chain still makes `state(oldest)` traverse every renewal via `head` before the expiry check (`:354` precedes `:357`). Repeated old-claim polling therefore scales with history. The new benchmark (`crates/hm-store/tests/lease.rs:161`) spaces events one minute apart and does not exercise either case. These are expired-data costs expressly retained in ADR-005's scope.
  Fix: bound/index historical overlap work while preserving deterministic outcomes, cache terminal renewal heads or short-circuit expired chains, and add dense-expired and long-expired-renewal benchmarks through Store/feed as well as LeaseBook.
- **N22 [MED] `crates/hm-store/src/settle.rs:232,243,331,365` — a query-time loser revives after a renewal is released.** Renewal's parent branch bypasses the rival-killer branch; later `walk` losses are transient rather than part of the signed-set fate.
  Cooperative trace, all paths [a]: Alice P=(L1, ts=T, TTL2s); Bob B=(L2, ts=T+3s, TTL10s); Alice R=(L3, ts=T+1s, TTL10s), renewing P. Two-second inter-node clock skew permits these signed times without malicious behavior. P and B do not coexist, so B enters `open`; R renews P and keeps its L1 priority, despite coexisting with B. At T+6s, B reports Lost to R. Alice releases R at L4/T+6s; B becomes Granted without a fresh claim.
  Fix: derive persistent losing fate for conflicts introduced by renewal, with a clearly specified deterministic replay rule. Test the Lost-to-Granted transition at a fixed `now`, including shuffled delivery, rather than only losers detected on their original insertion.
